Frequently asked questions

Select a question to see the answer.

How do I create an account?

Select Sign up in the header, enter your name, email and a password, then accept the terms. You can add a profile photo during sign up or later.

How do I build and print my resume?

Open the resume builder, fill in each section and choose a style. The preview updates as you type. When you are done, print the page and save it as a PDF.

How do I change the site's theme?

Go to the Themes page, pick the look you want and save. Your choice applies across every page.

How do I practice for an interview?

Open interview practice, choose a question and start the timer. Your AI coach gives feedback on each answer, and your past chats are saved in the history list.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Refresh the page first. If the problem continues, send us feedback below and choose Report a problem so we can look into it.
