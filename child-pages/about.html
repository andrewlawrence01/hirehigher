Our Mission

Hire Higher is designed to help job seekers build professional resumes, prepare for interviews, and discover new employment opportunities.

What We Offer

  • Resume building tools
  • AI-powered interview preparation
  • Job postings
  • Career preparation resources

Meet Our Team

Hire Higher is a project created by three students at Brigham Young University-Idaho. Each team member brings unique skills and responsibilities to the project. Together, we are working to create a platform that helps job seekers achieve their career goals.

Molli Batty

Product Manager

Molli oversees project planning and coordination, helping guide the team toward its goals and keeping the project organized throughout development. Molli implemented the AI chat bot that interviews for a job, she added the job posting feature, and she worked on adding a save and delete chat feature. She added emotion to the AI chat bot to gamify the interview experience and make it more realistic.

Andrew Lawrence

Product Designer

Andrew focuses on the design and user experience of Hire Higher, helping create an intuitive, consistent, and visually appealing platform. He was the one who worked on the homepage itself, the about page, account creation and password keeping, and the red-black theme.

Jace Kelley

Tech Lead

Jace guides the technical development of Hire Higher, helping oversee implementation and the technical direction of the project.
