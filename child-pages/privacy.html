Privacy & Security

Hire Higher is committed to protecting the information users provide when using our website.

Information We Collect

When creating an account, users may provide their first name, last name, and email address.

Users may also provide profile information, including their full name, location, biography, and profile photo.

Account Security

Hire Higher uses Supabase Authentication to manage user sign-in and account authentication.

Password authentication is handled through Supabase. Users should never share their passwords with other people.

Data Storage

Account and profile information is stored using Supabase services.

Profile photos are stored using Supabase Storage.

Questions About Privacy

If you have questions about the information you provide to Hire Higher, please contact the website administrators.
